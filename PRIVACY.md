# NightShift Privacy Policy

NightShift is built to respect your privacy completely.

## What we collect

Nothing. NightShift collects no personal data, no browsing history, and no
analytics.

## What leaves your browser

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. It has no servers and no
third-party services.

## What is stored, and where

Your settings — whether dark mode is on, which sites you've excluded, and your
brightness/contrast/warmth values — are saved using the browser's local
`storage` API on your own device. They never leave your machine. Removing the
extension removes them.

## Permissions, and why

- **storage** — to remember your settings between sessions.
- **activeTab / tabs** — to know the current site's address so the per-site
  toggle and shortcut can target it.
- **scripting / host access (`<all_urls>`)** — to apply dark-mode styling on
  the pages you visit. The styling runs locally; no page content is read,
  copied, or transmitted.
