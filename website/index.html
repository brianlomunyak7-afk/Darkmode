Install in a minute

  1. Download the extension: nightshift.zip, then unzip it (or clone the repo).
  2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin NightShift and click the moon to open the controls.

Works in any Chromium browser. Browser internal pages can't be themed by any extension.
